Hello! I'm a Post-Baccalaureate Researcher in the Department of Astronomy at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I graduated from the University of Illinois in May 2026 with a B.S. in Astrophysics and Data Science with Distinction, and I work with Professor Kirk Barrow in his Computational Cosmology group. My research focuses on galaxy formation and evolution using cosmological simulations, particularly how stellar populations shape galaxies.

Outside of research, I have a strong interest in astronomy education and outreach. I previously served as President of the University of Illinois Astronomical Society, where I organized public observing nights, workshops, and community events. I especially enjoy introducing people to the night sky through the historic University of Illinois Observatory.

I love astronomy not only for the questions it allows us to explore, but also for the sense of wonder that comes with understanding our universe. I hope to pursue a career in academia where I can continue researching, teaching, and sharing that excitement with others.
