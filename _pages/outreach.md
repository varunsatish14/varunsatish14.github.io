I believe that astronomy is best experienced when people have the opportunity to explore it for themselves. Whether through looking at the night sky for the first time or learning to operate a telescope, hands-on experiences can make science more accessible and engaging.

Throughout my time at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, I have been involved in astronomy outreach focusing on our local community. These experiences have shaped my interest in helping students and the broader community connect with astronomy.

## Astronomy Outreach & Community Engagement

### University of Illinois Astronomical Society

**President** · April 2024 – May 2026

As President of the University of Illinois Astronomical Society (UIAS), I helped lead the organization's outreach and community engagement activities. I organized public observing nights, observatory open houses, educational workshops, and events for incoming students and prospective astronomy majors.

Some of my responsibilities included:

- Organizing public observing nights and community star parties.
- Planning educational workshops and events for university students.
- Coordinating outreach activities for local schools and the general public.
- Supporting astronomy admissions events and student orientations.
- Developing interactive exhibits and improving public engagement initiatives.

### Friends of the University of Illinois Observatory

**Board Member** · April 2024 – May 2026

I served as the Astronomical Society representative on the board of Friends of the University of Illinois Observatory. Through this role, I supported efforts to preserve the historic Campus Observatory and strengthen its relationship with the broader community. I also initiated programs aimed at improving alumni connections and increasing community participation in observatory events and preservation efforts.

## Teaching

### Telescope Observing Teaching Assistant

**Department of Astronomy, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign** · August 2023 – May 2026

As a Telescope Observing Teaching Assistant, I helped students develop practical observational astronomy skills through hands-on experience with the historic University of Illinois Observatory telescope.

I trained more than 30 students in telescope operation and preservation and led eight semesters of nighttime and solar observing sessions for introductory astronomy courses.

My responsibilities included:

- Training students in telescope operation and observational techniques.
- Leading nighttime and solar observing sessions.

## STEM & Space Science Outreach

### Illinois Space Society

**Educational Outreach Board Member** · October 2022 – May 2024

As a member of the Illinois Space Society's Educational Outreach Board, I supported STEM initiatives connecting local schools with space science and engineering.

I helped coordinate interactive events and workshops for middle and high school students, developing educational activities designed to encourage early interest in astronomy, space exploration, and STEM careers.

## Looking Ahead

I hope to continue contributing to astronomy outreach and education throughout my research career.

I particularly enjoy creating opportunities for students to experience astronomy firsthand, whether through observational activities, educational programming, or undergraduate research.
